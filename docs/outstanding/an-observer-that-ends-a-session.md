| **An observer keeps a session running, and closing its own view can then end it.** A session ends when the last client that has it open closes it or disconnects, whatever that client's role. So when the only `user` client of a session leaves, an `observer` still watching keeps the session running and holding its lock, with nobody to drive it; and the observer's `session/close`, or its disconnect, then ends the session. "Everything that starts, changes or ends something is refused" for an observer ([../architecture/11-rpc-protocol.md](../architecture/11-rpc-protocol.md)), and closing its own view is allowed, so the two readings meet here. The choices: leave it (an observer that stays is what keeps a session alive for it to watch); end a session when its last client that may drive leaves, and tell the observers; or keep a session alive with observers only until a time limit.
