| **An interrupt of an idle session does not stop a summary still running in the background.** A summary prepared in the background outlives the turn that started it, and an interrupt stops it only while a turn runs ([07-sessions-and-context.md](../architecture/07-sessions-and-context.md), "In the background", as built in step 15). With no turn running, `interrupt` does nothing, so the request goes on until it ends or the session closes. Its cost is bounded by `summary_max_output` (16,384 tokens) and it is not lost: its usage is counted at the next boundary. To settle: have `interrupt` on an idle session stop a running summary, and count what it used ([a-summary-stopped-or-left-is-not-counted.md](a-summary-stopped-or-left-is-not-counted.md)). | Step 23 (the TUI) or step 27 (the RPC server) | When a front end offers an interrupt between turns |
