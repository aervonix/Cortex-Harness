| **A confined program cannot make a session of its own.** `setsid` is answered as done and not done, so that every process a command starts dies with its tree before the look at protected paths ([decision 0032](../decisions/0032-what-the-linux-sandbox-needs.md)). It costs every program that makes a terminal (the child of `forkpty` exits: Python's `pty.spawn`, pexpect, node-pty; `script` runs without one), and every program that signals the session it believes it made as a group (Node's `process.kill(-pid)` after `detached: true` finds none; Python's `os.killpg(os.getpgid(pid))` after `start_new_session=True` kills the whole command). The way out today is a run outside the sandbox with approval. Found by the second review of step 26, which also found that faking `setpgid` broke `timeout`; that one is allowed again. **Recommendation:** let `setsid` work, and catch a process that left the session another way: make the harness the child subreaper (`PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER`), so that what a command started is re-parented to the harness when its parent dies, and kill every child of the harness that is not one of its own known processes before the look that follows a command.
