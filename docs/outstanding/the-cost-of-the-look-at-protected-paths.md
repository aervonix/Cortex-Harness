| **What the look at protected paths costs in a large workspace.** Every confined command has its workspace walked before and after it for protected names at any depth ([architecture/06-permissions-and-sandbox.md](../architecture/06-permissions-and-sandbox.md), "As built in step 26"). Measured on the desktop, release build: 23.6 ms a look for 3,278 directories, so about 47 ms a command; a workspace past 20,000 directories (a monorepo with `node_modules`) reaches the bound, a quarter of a second a look, half a second a command, and every result then warns that the look was incomplete. It is not counted in the 5 ms budget for starting a command, which it would far pass. **Recommendation:** the after-look lists again only the directories whose change time moved since the before-look, and the before-look of a command reuses the after-look of the one before when nothing but the harness's own tools ran between them; both keep what is looked at the same. To be measured against a real monorepo before it is built. | Step 33 (performance pass) | Before the first release |
